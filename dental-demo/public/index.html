Four packages, four different jobs

Pricing is fitted to the practice and package. The full implementation scope is broader than this local demo.

PACKAGE 1 · WEBSITE CONVERSION

Conversion System

Turns visitors into enquiries. It does not talk or book.

  • Conversion-focused homepage or landing page, designed mobile-first
  • Clear call, quote and emergency actions; click-to-call and WhatsApp
  • Service and service-area pages, enquiry forms and trust signals
  • Call/form/button tracking and basic lead notifications

Your existing traffic reaches a clearer, faster path to contacting you.

PACKAGE 2 · CONFIRMED BOOKINGS

Booking Engine

Turns an enquiry into a confirmed time. The customer proposes three times; the practice picks one.

  • Everything in Conversion System
  • Callback details, service and urgency; photo upload where useful
  • Customer proposes three times; team chooses one
  • Confirmation to both sides, shared calendars, reminders and rescheduling

The customer can request an appointment now; you confirm the time that works.

PACKAGE 3 · 24/7 ENQUIRY HANDLING

AI Revenue System

Answers and qualifies enquiries when nobody is free, including after hours.

  • Everything in Booking Engine
  • Website AI text chat and voice with 24/7 replies
  • Missed-call capture and automated callback request
  • Qualification, urgency and service routing
  • Contact capture, team summary, CRM/lead-board entry and instant alerts
  • Source and conversion reporting

The website answers, qualifies and captures the enquiry when the team is unavailable.

PACKAGE 4 · END-TO-END REVENUE

Advanced AI Revenue

Runs the path from first contact to booked appointment, then follows up.

  • Everything in AI Revenue System
  • AI telephone receptionist, outbound follow-up and missed-call text-back
  • Emergency escalation and routing across teams or locations
  • Quote/photo collection and follow-up on open quotes and unbooked leads
  • Reactivation, review requests and CRM pipeline updates
  • Call recordings, structured summaries and monthly revenue review

The lead-to-booking path runs continuously, follows up and reports the revenue it brings in.

This prototype demonstrates sample chat, browser speech where supported, urgent/after-hours guidance, local callback and booking capture, illustrative time slots, and a local owner dashboard. Live calendars, telephony, outbound follow-up, CRM, notifications and production reporting are not connected.
